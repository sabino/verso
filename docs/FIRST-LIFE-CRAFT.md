# First life: creative direction and review

Three directions were considered: a busy living-world sandbox, a supernatural mystery that constantly interrupts ordinary work, and an intimate story about obligations inherited with a body. The third keeps the generated household, profession, relationships, paid work and eventual body transfer in one line of play. The wider simulation remains available without demanding attention at arrival.

> Verso believes a life is made of promises already in progress. It emphasizes listening, choosing, working and living with the result. The world should feel inhabited and legible; interactions should have small physical and social consequences. Motion should have preparation and recovery while pixels remain crisp. Sound should leave room for the world and for silence. It avoids a screen of systems masquerading as purpose.

The baseline published first life at seed 8 began beside a named acquaintance, with two rations and an obligation to deliver five. The large chat area and dense sidebar competed with the scene. The quest panel described the delivery but did not explain whose account was at stake. The merchant dialogue initially showed stock and a generic trade introduction even when that merchant was part of the resident's case. Camera follow rounded its own feedback state every frame, which can prevent a slow camera movement from accumulating.

The opening now asks the player to hear two real named people before choosing an account. Trust changes faction standing and the journal; then the panel follows the existing household delivery and paid commission system. Its stages are derived from saved facts, so continuing an older save does not create a second quest log. Merchant dialogue keeps the person's case in view. The current concern sits above the map; solo chat starts compact, with a voice-settings entry but without inactive microphone controls. Case conversations show consequential actions first and disclose optional social/trade actions on request. Character nameplates avoid each other and leave the player visible. Camera interpolation retains fractional progress and snaps only its projection. Work poses have preparation, a contact peak and recovery; wood and stone contacts have different fragments, and their Foley is timed to the tool's contact. Safe wilderness music sometimes yields completely to ambience; home and temple phrases leave out percussion unless the scene becomes active.

The PR browser artifact captures the same seed and resident before and after the change. It also records native-input conversations, the trust decision and a frame-work sample. A short WAV audition of four deterministic score contexts uses the same phrase composer and instrument tables. It is a score-only preview; the live Web Audio mix adds filtering, ambience, Foley and reverb. These are repeatable review scenes, not a substitute for perceptual listening or a long human playthrough. Body silhouettes, every world biome, later combat and the full score still require sustained art and listening passes before the larger overhaul can honestly be called complete.
